The problem

Most small-business sales that fall apart do so during due diligence, and usually over paperwork: a tax return that doesn't match the books, a lease nobody checked, or a lien from a loan paid off years ago.

Every week a buyer waits for a document is a week for them to rethink the price. Sellers who have everything ready keep the deal moving and keep the buyer's trust.

The 96-document checklist

Financials, taxes, legal, contracts, real estate, equipment, employees, customers, operations, insurance, debt, and intellectual property. Each line tells you what the buyer is checking for, so you know why it matters.

Red flags to fix first

Ten problems that lower offers or end deals, each with what to do about it before you list.

A preparation timeline

A data room folder plan

A document request log

Who it's for

  • Owners with no plans to sell who want to be ready if an offer comes
  • Owners planning to sell in the next 6 to 24 months
  • Owners who already have a buyer and need to get organized fast
  • Owners selling without a broker, or who want to be prepared before hiring one

Who it's not for

This is not a substitute for an attorney or an accountant, and it won't value your business. It gets your documents in order so that their time, and your money, goes to the deal instead of to finding paperwork.
